BUBBLE SHOOTER

Match • Shoot • Score!

🎮 HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Aim the shooter at the bubbles.
🫧 Your current bubble is shown at the bottom.
🔮 The next bubble is shown beside it.
💥 Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.
⭐ Earn points for every successful match.
❤️ You have 3 lives.
⏱️ Finish before the timer reaches zero!

📚 DSA USED

📚 STACK — stores shot history (LIFO)

🚶 QUEUE — stores upcoming bubbles (FIFO)

🔗 LINKED LIST — stores popped-bubble history

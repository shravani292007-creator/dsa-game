CASTLE DEFENSE

Defend the castle from incoming enemies!

⚔️ THE MISSION

Enemies are approaching your castle. Tap them to attack before they reach the castle!

Survive the waves and get the highest score.



📖 HOW TO PLAY

👾 Enemies:
Enemies will appear on the battlefield and move toward your castle.

🏹 Attack:
Tap an enemy to shoot it with an arrow.

⭐ Score:
Every defeated enemy gives you points.

❤️ Castle Health:
If an enemy reaches your castle, you lose health.

🌊 Waves:
Defeat enemies to complete each wave.

⏱️ Timer:
Survive until all waves are completed.


📚 DSA CONCEPTS

📚 STACK — LIFO

Our attacks are stored in a Stack.

Last attack added → First attack removed

Example:
🏹 Attack 3
🏹 Attack 2
🏹 Attack 1

🚶 QUEUE — FIFO

Incoming enemies wait in a Queue.

First enemy arriving → First enemy handled

👾 Enemy 1 → 👾 Enemy 2 → 👾 Enemy 3

🔗 LINKED LIST

Every defeated enemy is added to a Linked List.

Enemy → Enemy → Enemy → NULL
